ACT I

The house is dark before the show even starts

The Problem
01

Scattered casting

Character breakdowns and audition notes live in spreadsheets no one keeps current.

02

Buried call sheets

Rehearsal and call information travels through long email chains, easy to miss.

03

Paper cue books

Cues for lighting, sound, and props sit in separate binders that fall out of sync.

04

Untracked donors

Donor history gets tracked in someone's personal notes, if it's tracked at all.

05

Budget guesswork

Department spend is discovered after the fact, not managed while it happens.

Every department is telling the same story from a different script — and no one in the building is reading the same page.

ACT II

One production, one workspace

The Platform
DASHBOARD

Command view

Permission-based home base: active productions, upcoming shows, financial summaries, casting, and rehearsals at a glance.

CONTACTS

One shared directory

Every actor, donor, board member, and vendor in one database, tagged, importable, and tied to real contribution history.

PRODUCTION & SCENE BUILDER

The source of truth

Directors build each scene once — time, location, action, mood, music — and it feeds every creative department automatically.

DEPARTMENT TOOLS

A workspace per craft

Lighting, sound, wardrobe, props, and set each get purpose-built tools connected directly to the scene they belong to.

VOLUNTEER MANAGEMENT

End-to-end scheduling

Opportunity postings, shift scheduling, applications, a public sign-up portal, and check-in with hours tracking.

CALENDARS

Every schedule, filtered

Audition, rehearsal, show, and board calendars — filterable by production, department, or date range.

FINANCIALS & BOARD

Numbers boards trust

Budgets, donor contributions, ticket sales, and grants, with department-level variance visible to the people who need it.

GHOSTLIGHT

The assistant that watches the whole show

It won't design your set, light your show, or tell you how to block a scene. Those calls are yours, always. GhostLight handles the grant letters, donor records, prop tracking, and paperwork, so you have more time for the parts only an artist can do.

ROLE-BASED ACCESS

The right view for every seat

Admins, directors, managers, crew, board, and cast each see a workspace scoped to exactly what their role requires.

ACT III

Built from a single script

How It Works
Universal Source of Truth

Scene Builder

A director defines a scene once. Every department below reads from that data and writes back to it. Costume changes, prop swaps, and blocking notes flow both ways, so the scene stays current everywhere at once, with no re-entry and no drift.

ACT V

The next act is already blocked

The Roadmap
01

Dramaturgy

A dramaturgy builder tied to each production, so period and script research reaches wardrobe and props before those decisions get made.

02

Ticketing & revenue

Ticket sales pulled in from your ticketing system and factored straight into revenue reporting and P&L.

03

Calendar sync

Two-way sync with Google Calendar, so rehearsal and show dates update in both places at once.

04

Script AI

Upload a script and let it map scenes to the Scene Builder and rough out a budget and rehearsal schedule.

05

Onboarding wizard

A guided walkthrough for first login, tailored to each role, that remembers where you left off.

06

Messaging & notifications

Send audition invites actors can accept, pick a date from, and have auto-added to the production calendar.

07

Rehearsal note tagging

Directors can @ mention an actor or department in rehearsal notes and route them straight to that person's or department's private space.

08

Auto-connect by location

Performers and crew who opt in during signup get automatically connected to new theatres in their area.

None of it replaces the person doing the work. It just means the tool already knows what they're walking into.
